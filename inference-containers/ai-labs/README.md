# Open-Source / Open-Weight AI Labs — Model Profiles

A directory of the companies and labs shipping open (open-weight or fully-open) AI models,
with profiles of their release cadence, model sizes, licensing/openness posture, and how the
models are served locally. Each lab has its own report (linked below), generated by web-verified
research and kept to a common template (`_TEMPLATE.md`).

These profiles are the *upstream* source of the models this repository actually serves. The
`inference-containers/` tree currently runs a subset of them on two hardware targets:

- **RTX 5090** (AMD64 / Blackwell consumer GPU, WSL2)
- **NVIDIA DGX Spark / GB10** (ARM64 Grace Blackwell, 128 GB unified memory)

> All facts in the per-lab reports were web-verified at write time (2026-07-19). Model
> names/sizes/licences beyond the assistant's training cutoff are cited to live sources in each
> report's References section. Re-verify before building on any specific claim.

## Openness spectrum

- **Fully open** — releases weights *and* training data, code, and logs: **AI2 / OLMo** (the only one here).
- **Open-weight, permissive** — Apache-2.0 / MIT weights, no data/code: Mistral, DeepSeek, Qwen, Gemma (since v4), Zhipu/GLM, Thinking Machines, Moonshot (Modified MIT).
- **Open-weight, restricted license** — open weights but with field-of-use/redistribution terms: NVIDIA (Nemotron Open Model License).
- **Partial / closing frontier** — small & mid models open, frontier flagships API-only: Qwen (Qwen3.6-Max/Plus, Qwen3.7 closed), Moonshot (K1.5 closed; K3 weights delayed).

## How these reports were produced

Each lab profile was researched by an isolated sub-agent with an empty context, web-verifying
every factual claim (sizes, dates, licenses, serving support) and writing to a fixed Markdown
template. Reports were produced in three parallel waves (4 + 4 + 1) and audited for structure,
depth, and source citation before being linked here. The template used is `_TEMPLATE.md`.

## Template

All reports follow `_TEMPLATE.md`: YAML frontmatter (`lab`, `slug`, `openness`,
`local_support`, `updated`, `notable_models`) plus the sections Overview, Release Cadence,
Models & Sizes, Openness Status, Serving, References, and Local Deployment in This Repo.
