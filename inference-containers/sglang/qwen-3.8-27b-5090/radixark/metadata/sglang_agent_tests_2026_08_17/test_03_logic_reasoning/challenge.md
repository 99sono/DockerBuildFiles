# Challenge 03: Logic puzzle

Solve the following logic puzzle carefully. State your assumptions and the
deduction steps you used.

**The Puzzle**

Five houses sit in a row, painted five different colors: red, blue, green,
yellow, and white. In each house lives a person of a different nationality:
Brit, Swede, Dane, Norwegian, and German. Each drinks a different drink:
tea, coffee, milk, beer, and water. Each has a different pet: dog, cat, bird,
horse, and fish. Each smokes a different brand: Dunhill, Blend, Pall Mall,
Prince, and Blue Master.

1. The Brit lives in the red house.
2. The Swede keeps a dog as a pet.
3. The Dane drinks tea.
4. The green house is immediately to the left of the white house (the green house is on the left side of the white house).
5. The owner of the green house drinks coffee.
6. The person who smokes Pall Mall keeps a bird.
7. The owner of the yellow house smokes Dunhill.
8. The man living in the house right in the center drinks milk.
9. The Norwegian lives in the first house (leftmost).
10. The man who smokes Blend lives next to the one who keeps a cat.
11. The man who keeps a horse lives next to the one who smokes Dunhill.
12. The man who smokes Blue Master drinks beer.
13. The German smokes Prince.
14. The Norwegian lives next to the blue house.
15. The man who smokes Blend has a neighbor who drinks water.

**Questions**

1. Who drinks water?
2. Who owns the fish?

Write your full solution, including a final grid/table, into `answer.md`.
